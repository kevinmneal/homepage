A closer look at the way home +
A place in this puzzle has three parts: square, heading, and allowed turn. Come back facing another way, or allowed to turn the other way, and a route that was closed can open.
That is why a familiar square can become useful again. The route you take to a switch also determines the direction you face when its rule takes effect.
The local solver follows the same two moves you do, comparing complete states. You can ask it to check a prediction or reveal a route; it all happens in your browser.
